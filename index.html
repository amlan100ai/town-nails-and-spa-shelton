Our Story

Nearly two decades on Bridgeport Avenue

Town Nail & Spa has operated in Shelton since being founded in August 2006 — coming up on twenty years serving the same neighborhood on Bridgeport Avenue.

The salon's Better Business Bureau file has been open since 2008, and its storefront still carries the full original service lineup — manicures, pedicures, nail extensions, gel, massage, reflexology, facials, and ear candling — all in one spacious location with a full row of pedicure chairs and manicure stations.

Today, Town Nail & Spa is reachable by phone, in person, or through its Facebook page — with no independently branded website of its own yet.
